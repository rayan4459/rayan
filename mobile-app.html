    FAQ

    Questions people ask before they tap.

    What if I lose the ring?

    Open Manage-Mii and switch the token off: the ring stops paying instantly, and it can only be managed from your account, so nobody else can re-activate it. Your card itself is untouched — no need to call your bank. Found it again? Switch it back on.

    Does the ring store my card number?

    No. When you add your card, its details are encrypted on your phone and validated by your bank; the ring only ever receives a payment token — a substitute number that works nowhere else. Losing the ring never exposes your card.

    Can I add several cards, or use several wearables?

    Yes to both. Every wearable you link — ring, bracelet, keyring — is bound to your account and carries its own token, managed from the same screen. Each wearable holds one active card at a time; swap it whenever you like.

    Does it need charging or a phone nearby?

    Neither. The ring has no battery: the terminal powers it at the moment you pay. Your phone is only needed to set it up, to add or freeze a card — not at the checkout.

    Can I shower or swim with it?

    Yes. The ring is sealed ceramic with no port and no battery; water, soap and sweat don't affect it. Take it off for saunas and hot tubs, as with any jewellery.

    I'm changing phones. What happens?

    Install Manage-Mii on the new phone and sign in to your account: your wearables and tokens follow you. The ring keeps paying in the meantime.

    Which size should I choose?

    Measure it on screen in two minutes with our ring sizer (US, EU and UK sizes), or ask for the free sizer kit. Between two sizes, take the larger one for wide knuckles, the smaller one for slim fingers.

    Do you need any help?

    Questions about pairing, tokens or your bank's onboarding — write to us, a human answers.

Payment-enabled wearables

Integrating a payment-certified secure element, these wearables enable contactless payments with a simple tap. Compatible with any terminal in the world, wearables bring payments to new levels of convenience.

Specialized wearables

Your imagination and needs are the only limit — from fitness rings to healthcare and emergency contactless bracelets, to branded event wearables that augment the guest experience.

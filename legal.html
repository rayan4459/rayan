Privacy policy

We collect as little as possible, and only to answer you.

What we collect, and why

  • Contact form. Your name, email address, message and — depending on what you ask — your company, the product you are interested in, an estimated volume, your ring size and finish. Purpose: replying to your request, preparing a quote or a consultation. Legal basis: the steps you ask us to take before a contract, and our legitimate interest in answering you.
  • Email. If you write to hello@curiolab.me directly, we keep the exchange for the same purpose.
  • Nothing else. No account, no newsletter, no advertising identifiers, no analytics script on this site.

Who receives it

The CurioLab team. The contact form is delivered by our form provider, provider name to complete when the endpoint is configured, which processes the message on our behalf and does not use it for its own purposes. No data is sold or shared with third parties for marketing.

How long we keep it

Requests are kept for 12 months after our last exchange, then deleted — unless they lead to an order, in which case the order records are kept for the duration required by accounting law.

Where it is processed

Our team is based in the United Arab Emirates. If you write from the European Union, your data leaves the EU; the form provider named above provides the appropriate safeguards for that transfer.

Cookies & local storage

This site sets no cookies. It uses your browser's local storage for two conveniences, which never leave your device:

  • curio-theme — whether you chose the light or dark theme.
  • curio-sizer — the screen calibration and ring size you found with the sizer, and the finish you picked, so the other pages can show them back to you.

You can clear both at any time from your browser settings (site data for curiolab.me).

Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct or delete the data we hold about you, to limit its use, or to receive a copy of it. Write to hello@curiolab.me — we answer within one month. If you are in the European Union you may also lodge a complaint with your national data protection authority.

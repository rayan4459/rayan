    Everything around your ring.

    Six things worth knowing before it's on your finger.

    • Any contactless terminal in the world, confirmed in under a second. No phone to unlock, no card to find — just your hand.

    • The terminal powers the ring at the moment you pay. Nothing to charge, ever. Shower, swim, sleep with it on.

    • Lost it, lent it? Switch the ring's payment token off from the Manage-Mii app. It stops paying instantly; your card is untouched. Switch it back on whenever you like.

    • Black ceramic, brushed titanium, rose gold, midnight blue. One silhouette: a satin band framed by two mirror-polished bevels, your name engraved inside.

    • Calibrate your screen with a bank card, then compare the ring at true size with your finger or a ring you already wear. Two minutes, no tools. US, EU and UK sizes.

    • Still unsure? We post you a ring sizer before shipping your Curio, free of charge. Try it for a day: fingers change with the weather and the hour.
